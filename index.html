Md Tahrim Kobir Riyad

B.Sc. Agriculture (Hons.) student & Research Assistant, Sylhet Agricultural University

I study how plants respond to abiotic stress by combining physiology experiments with computational genomics. As a research assistant at Sylhet Agricultural University, I work across functional genomics, enzymatic kinetics and structural bioinformatics. I am preparing for postgraduate research in plant stress biology, crop improvement and climate-resilient agriculture.
